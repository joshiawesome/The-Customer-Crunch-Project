The below Venn diagram is intended to provide an overreaching view of the inter-realtionship between each Insight Driver of the CDC DISCOVERY classification.

DATA MANAGEMENT directly relates to effectiveness on how data is stored and processed; ANALYTICAL CAPABILITY
infers how well data is assessed and transformed to be released to the business, finally INSIGHT ACTIVATION provides a view on the value of insight, to be consumed for strategic planning or actioned for immediate use on tactical programs.

These results on their own can help you identify the areas where further investigation and effort is required. In contrast, the overlap areas can indicate friction points, which could reflect process or communication inefficiencies.

FRICTION POINTS

Analysis Driven Culture (/) :

This is the point of where data is transformed into insights. When working optimally data is
clearly understood, is regularly available, and of high quality.

Insight Driven Culture (/) :

Is the point of effective translation of data into insights, it must be structured to
communicate in a format that supports strategic decisions or enables insight lead actions

Data Driven Culture (/) :

Working effectively the business is capturing customer interactions, processing this data, and
provisioning to the insights team or tools at speed. This allows enables a more effective
response time to customer needs.
